Urban Life

Description

This project is a 2D urban city life animation developed using C++, OpenGL and GLUT that represents the city life. The scene features uttara university, young boys playing in the field, trees, a ferris wheel, emergency vehicle, truck, car and airplane. It includes both Day and Night modes, where during the day the sun appears, vehicles move continuously, and two boys play with a ball, while at night the sky becomes dark, the moon appears, building windows light up, vehicle headlights turn on and the play field is empty. the traffic signals control the vehicle movements like the real life traffic signal.

Skills Used: C++, OpenGL, GLUT, 2D Graphics, Animation, Object Movement, Keyboard Interaction
