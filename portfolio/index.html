About

Fatima Hridi is a Computer Science and Engineering student at American Internationational University-Bangladesh with a curious mind and a creative heart. Since childhood, she has always wanted to be an engineer, but her love for creativity was just as strong. From a young age, she enjoyed drawing, storytelling and trying out creative hobbies like origami and calligraphy. During her college days, she discovered a passion for volunteering, organizing events and connecting with people. She chose Computer Science because she wants to build things that can make a difference. Her dream is to use her skills to help people through projects that serve communities in need. She wants her journey as an engineer to not just be about technology, but about making life better—for people, for nature, for the world.
